Callouts

  1. Spectrum tile — worst of PNT denial, cyber pressure, space-weather ops impact and edge exposure. UNKNOWN is its own tone. Click → Watch › I&W.
  2. PNT denial — GPSJam cells in the AOI bbox vs the AOI's own 30-day baseline + GPS/WAAS system NOTAMs + RAIM at hubs. Restores GPS to the templated boards (it was dropped). The storm guard attributes to weather first.
  3. Cyber pressure — four legs: IODA national outage alerts, ransomware victims vs baseline, graded cyber news BY the actor, CISA advisories that name a state actor. Own-source-only caps at Watch, as everywhere.
  4. Space activity — only on boards whose actor launches. CelesTrak SATCAT + SOCRATES, Launch Library 2. Learning mode until the cadence baseline forms.
  5. SITREP Spectrum card — the wing-level view: PNT at the field, HF/GPS/SATCOM impact from SWPC with plain language, KEV hits on declared vendors, 3-day outlook. Its LED feeds LIMFAC C2 / Comms and the HTML export.
  6. Space weather → ops — the existing card reframed. Environment, not warning: it never raises an I&W level; it earns a Threat-board row only at scale 3+.
  7. Digital & spectrum — the Regional dossier's new card, same sources sliced by country.
  8. Outages layer — IODA country events at centroid; popup names the sources that saw it.
  9. Launches layer — pads with T-minus; a launch window is an airspace closure.
  10. ⌁ Cyber instrument — classes: disruptive · ransom / criminal · espionage / pre-positioning · DDoS / hacktivist. A CISA state-attributed advisory is a BY act with Federal-Register standing.
  11. Declarations — three optional fields in the Mission Profile. Nothing per person; nothing sent to a model.
