# Design review, October 2026 — findings

> Plan: `PLAN-DESIGN-REVIEW.md`. One section per surface, each from a
> walkthrough the operator recorded (screens + a written user story), read
> against the code. Format follows `REVIEW.md`: finding → cause → verdict →
> proposal → size. Verdicts: **Fix** (a defect), **Change** (a design
> choice to revise), **Build** (new capability), **Keep**. A status line at
> the end of each section tracks what shipped.

## 1. Glance (walkthrough 2026-10-05, desktop, 14:27 EDT while TDY in Jordan)

### What the walkthrough says works — Keep

- The clock wall: time, date, daylight phase in one read.
- The brief's Key developments and Suggested focus: relevant to current
  missions and CENTCOM airfields, and it flags closures and the strategic
  pieces worth the time. The F-35/China item was noticed from here.
- "Not looked at since it worsened": it keyed a look. Keep it exactly where
  it is.
- Global Reach Watch: the right airfields and airspace, consistent with the
  other modules.
- Breaking & Critical with the Thesis button: used several times in one
  sitting.

### Findings

**G1 — Zulu belongs at the far left, the rest in UTC order.** *Change, S.*
`renderClocks` sorts every tile west→east by current offset, so New Jersey
(UTC−4) lands left of Zulu. Pin the UTC tile first; sort the remainder by
offset as now. One line in `lib/worldClocks.ts`, one test.

**G2 — The status row has no name.** *Change, S.* The eight tiles sit under
the brief with no heading, so the walkthrough called it "the section below
it". A small label ("RIGHT NOW · live, 5-min") and one line of help make
it legible as the instrument panel it is.

**G3 — Posture tile lands on Russia.** *Change, S–M.* The tile emits
`osint:set-pane regional`, and the Regional rail default-selects its first
worst-severity entry — Russia, RED and chronic for twelve days, the least
newsworthy RED on the board. Land on the *most relevant* location instead:
the one that escalated today, else the TDY country (Jordan today), else the
hub's country, else the worst. The tile already knows all four.

**G4 — Spectrum says Taiwan, click goes to CENTCOM.** *Fix, S.* The tile's
label comes from the cyber indicator's own board, and its click sends
`watch:focus {kind:"iw", id:<that board>}` — but the Watch pane's handler
only opens the I&W panel (`setIwOpen(true)`) and discards the id, so the
panel shows its first board, CENTCOM · Iran. The I&W tile has the same
defect; it merely happened to point at the first board today. Fix:
`WarningBoard` takes a `focusProblemId` and expands and scrolls to it.

**G5 — Demand tile stuck on "loading".** *Fix, M.* Two causes. The status
row awaits all five routes in one `Promise.all`, so the slowest gates every
tile, and a route that fails (a 502 from the gateway on a cold
`getDemandHorizon` fan-out) leaves its tile at "…" until the five-minute
poll. And `/api/demand-horizon` awaits the bare assembly — the one route on
the row that does not follow the bounded-wait / `pending` contract the
spectrum and economy routes use. Fix both: fetch per tile with `allSettled`
and a short retry, and give the demand route `maxWaitMs` + `pending` so a
cold start answers in 8 s with the last body.

**G6 — Alerts before Tasks and Family.** *Change, S.* Reorder the tiles:
Posture · Bases · I&W · Spectrum · Demand · **Alerts** · Tasks · Family.

**G7 — Tasks and Family tooltips say nothing.** *Fix, S.* Both carry a
fixed sentence while every other tile lists its items. `/api/family/week`
already returns lapsed and due-soon titles with days-until; the Glance tab
holds the due tasks. Pass the item lists into the tiles and show them.

**G8 — Needs You Now buries the operator's own items.** *Change, S–M.*
Tasks are pinned in a violet "Your actions" group at the top (right), but
high-priority emails are ranked into the world list (rank 2–3) beneath
force-protection RED rows (rank −2) — the Google security alert sat at the
bottom. The operator's rule is clear: *things for me first, in my colour;
the world after, in its colours.* Move priority emails into "Your actions"
under the tasks, rendered violet; leave force, weather, disasters and OSINT
in the world list in red/amber. The "View all" target logic is unaffected.

**G9 — Which airfields matter most.** *Build, M.* Global Reach Watch ranks
by score, so an ordered departure anywhere outranks a NOTAM at the hub.
The Mission Profile already declares hub and spokes, and a trip declares
where the operator is. Pin rows that touch the hub, a spoke or the TDY
location to the top with a small "mine" mark, then the rest by score. No
new data; one ranking rule, tested.

**G10 — Tomorrow's event shows 3:00 AM; it is at 10:00 IDT.** *Fix, M.*
Not a bug in the arithmetic — 10:00 IDT (UTC+3) is 03:00 EDT, and the
device zone is New York (the clock wall marks it), so Glance and the
brief's schedule both rendered the right instant in the wrong zone for
where the operator is. Three parts: (a) **label the zone** on every time
on Glance's Today/Tomorrow and in the brief's schedule — a bare "3:00 AM"
is the real defect; (b) in Auto mode, **an active trip's zone wins** over
the device zone — the trip has coordinates, Open-Meteo's `timezone=auto`
(already used for the trip's weather) returns the IANA zone, store it on
the trip; Pin still overrides; (c) the tooltip shows the instant in both
zones. *Decision:* confirm that a declared TDY should set the effective
zone in Auto mode.

**G11 — A major posture change was nearly missed.** *Build, M–L.* "B-1s
Empty Out of Fairford" appeared as one newsletter line in On Your Radar.
A deployment, redeployment, surge, bed-down or withdrawal of aircraft is
exactly the demand signal the north star asks for, and nothing in the app
recognises the shape. Proposal: a deterministic **force-posture-move
grammar** (phrases, never words — the `economicWarfare.ts` discipline:
"deploy", "redeploy", "empty out", "stand up", "bed down", "surge",
"forward-deploy", "withdraw" paired with an airframe, unit or base, plus
an AOR resolution) over articles and newsletter bullets → a **Posture
moves** strip on Glance above Breaking, and a recency-decayed, capped
driver on the demand horizon for the AOR named. Falsifier and provenance
like every other sensor. *Decision:* build it in this pass or park it for
the Build phase.

**G12 — The Markets block is six static tickers.** *Change, S–M.* It
informs nothing. Two options: a live mini-ticker (price + day change) using
the Yahoo chart parser already in `lib/energyPrices.ts`, cached 15 min; or
cut it and show Brent plus the chokepoint lead-class strip, which is the
economic signal that actually bears on lift. *Decision:* ticker or cut.

**G13 — The OE brief should open, not download.** *Change, M.* The button
builds the zero-script HTML and forces a download. Open it in a new tab
instead, inside a thin viewer with two buttons — Download HTML and Print /
Save as PDF (the browser's print dialog) — while the exported file stays
script-free. Layout fixes found reading today's export:
- The masthead's AO line is one 300-character run-on; render it as a
  compact list (hub · spokes · theaters · AOIs with their chokepoints).
- "snapshot was stale at export" needs its meaning beside it ("built from a
  cached picture older than 5 min; nothing here is live") and must not sit
  next to a BLUF chip that says "no level change since last look".
- BLUF chips → a four-column line (RED · boards elevated · demand rising ·
  alerts) so it reads at a glance.
- Active alerts grouped by kind (force RED · ordered departure · I&W ·
  cyber) rather than one fourteen-item list.
- I&W boards print the indicator *taxonomy* text; print the current
  **drivers** instead — the taxonomy is the same every day.
- "Mobility commander's declared AO" → the operator's role.
- Team state "No crew state declared — posture against demand cannot be
  judged" is correct and should stay; it is the one line that asks the
  operator to do something.

**G14 — Data hygiene the walkthrough exposed.** *Operator action, S.* The
INDOPACOM AOI is named "Tawain" and the Ukraine AOI's primary list includes
"Stalingrad"; both are Mission Profile entries and both print on every
surface (I&W boards, alerts, the OE brief). Rename in Preferences →
Mission Profile. The app should not auto-correct declared names.

**G15 — The brief said King Hussein "de-escalates to AMBER"; the posture
table says it escalated today.** *Fix, S.* The deterministic record
(`previousComposite` lower than today's AMBER) is the truth; the prose is
the model reading "(was GREEN yesterday)" as a fall. The force line in the
prompt should state direction explicitly — "ESCALATED from GREEN" /
"EASED from RED" — so the model cannot invert it, and the brief's modal
"Today's Schedule" should not be headed *Today* when it holds only
tomorrow's item.

## 2. News — Feed · Threads · History (walkthrough 2026-10-05, desktop, 15:40 EDT)

Mockups first, as asked: `docs/news-threads.png` (the proposed landing view)
and `docs/news-read.png` (the proposed Read view), from
`docs/mockups/news-threads.html` / `news-read.html`. Numbered callouts on
each render match the proposals below.

### What the walkthrough says works — Keep

- Newsletters: real value — they carry current events and point at sources
  the operator trusts (Politico, A&SF). The problem is volume, not content.
- Threads: "the thing I find the most value in" — the balance of strategic
  depth and current events, with the AMC sentence at the end of each
  summary. Keep the model, the through-line, the per-thread summaries and
  the newsletter quote box exactly as they are.
- Glance → Breaking & critical remains the busy-day must-read path; nothing
  here should weaken it.

### What the operator is reading for

Two things, stated plainly in the story, and the tab should be organised
around them rather than around source categories:

1. **Strategic depth or insight** — long-form analysis (War on the Rocks,
   Foreign Affairs, the War Zone features).
2. **Current events that touch an AMC operational leader** — posture,
   chokepoints, pay/OPTEMPO tells, allied deliveries, HADR.

Everything else is "nice to have". Today the Feed shows all three mixed in
one grid behind eight category tabs, and the second thing the operator
wants (Threads) is a second page.

### Findings

**N1 — Threads is on the second page; it should be the first.** *Change, M.*
The News tab lands on Feed (newsletters, then the trending strip, then the
category grid). The view the operator values most is a click away and
greyed until the feed has loaded. Make Threads the landing view. Cost rule
(the AI-spend discipline): `/api/threads` is the most expensive call in the
app (Opus over ~40 articles, about $0.42) and is already day-cached by
article hash, so a Threads-first landing costs one call per distinct article
set per day. Add the brief's generation cap (3 per day unless ↻ Regenerate)
so a feed that moves through the day cannot spend more than that
unattended; past the cap, serve the last session and say so.

**N2 — Three views where two will do: fold History into Threads.** *Change,
M.* History holds the best data on the tab (label trajectories, sustained
escalation, re-emergence, the search over every past thread) and nobody
reaches it. The walkthrough asked both for "trending sorted by rising and
falling" and for History "to better inform me"; both are the same
proposal. Signals becomes a **Moving** rail beside the threads, sorted
sustained-escalation → rising → re-emerging → steady → fading, each row with
its run ("3rd day") and a 14-day sparkline. Labels becomes the sparkline +
run on every thread card (N4) and the drawer that opens from a Moving row.
Sessions becomes the date picker in the header ("▾ Oct 5") and two "Past
days" rows at the foot of the rail. The search box stays, in the drawer.
Two views remain: **Threads** and **Read**.

**N3 — The through-line buries the AMC sentence; no diff against
yesterday.** *Change, S.* The model already ends the through-line with
"For AMC: …"; render that sentence in the accent colour so it reads first.
Add one line under it — "vs yesterday: Bab el-Mandeb is NEW; Iran war
stable → rising; Ukraine unchanged" — computed by a pure diff of today's
session against the previous one (same `thread_sessions` rows History
reads). No model call.

**N4 — Thread cards carry no history.** *Build, M.* Each card shows an
arrow the model chose today and nothing else. Join the card to its label's
history: the sparkline, the run ("↑ Rising · 3rd day", "new today",
"fading"), and a **Δ yesterday** line (trend change, sources added or
dropped, headline shift) from the previous session's thread with the same
label. Pure, from stored sessions; `getLabelHistory` already exists.

**N5 — "So what · AMC" is a grey sentence at the end of a paragraph.**
*Change, S.* Split the model's final AMC sentence out of the summary into
its own accented line on the card (prompt: a separate `amc` field, one
sentence; fallback: the last sentence when it names AMC/mobility/lift).

**N6 — A thread cannot be acted on.** *Build, M–L.* The data the operator
wants to exploit has no doors out of the card. Four, all on things the app
already has: **Ask about this** (seeds the assistant with the thread —
N10); **Save to Docs** (creates or appends a 🧵 `thread` doc whose "Trace"
section the Docs tab already renders as a timeline — one entry per day:
date, trend, headline, sources; re-saving appends, never duplicates);
**Follow** (adds the label to the watchlist, which already pins matching
threads ⚑ and feeds Glance/alerts); and a door-in chip to the surface the
thread touches — the I&W board whose AOI it names, the chokepoint read, the
demand-horizon row (deterministic name match on label + AOI countries).

**N7 — Trending is unsorted and unlabelled.** *Change, S.* `TrendStrip`
renders the ten non-steady movers in API order with a glyph each. Group and
order them: ▲ rising (by velocity), ✦ new, ▼ fading; show count and ×
velocity; make a chip filter the lanes below (dispatch `docs:search`-style
into the feed filter). `classifyMovers` already computes state and
velocity.

**N8 — The newsletter stack.** *Change, M.* Fourteen unread rows, oldest
five days, with "13 series you've never opened" above them; the digest
button sits at the right of the heading. Reframe the section as a **queue**:
per-source unread counts and the age of the oldest; **Catch me up** (the
existing Digest) as the primary action; **Mark all read** for the days
there is no time; and by default only the rows that *earned* one — a
watchlist hit (⚑ houthis) or a thread match (thread · Iran war), with the
reason on the row — the routine rest behind "show all". Nothing is hidden
from the digest. The "never opened" line moves to the footer as "review
sources".

**N9 — One grid, eight tabs; the two things the operator reads for are not
lanes.** *Change, M.* Replace the primary category tabs with two lanes:
**Strategic depth** — long-form from the analysis sources (WOTR, Foreign
Affairs, CSIS, RAND, Lawfare, War Zone features), decided by source + body
length, deterministic, with read time — and **Operational now** — today's
AI-curated "Critical for you" with the AMC reason on the card (the curation
prompt already judges relevance; ask it for the reason in six words). Each
card names the thread it belongs to (article ids are on the thread). The
category tabs survive as filter chips under Trending. **Everything else
folds** under one heading with its count — the existing "More to discover"
affordance, generalised.

**N10 — Two assistants on one tab.** *Change, M.* The right-hand "News
analyst" (`NewsChatPanel` → `/api/news-chat`) and the floating assistant
(`FloatingAssistant` → `/api/chat`) are both Opus with a system prompt and
a context block; they differ only in what they are handed. The analyst has
40 articles, the newsletters and the threads, a reader persona, and a
"remember more/less of" instruction; no calendar, no OE picture, no actions,
no memory, and the conversation dies when the tab changes. The assistant
has calendar, tasks, 20 articles, 10 newsletters, the OE snapshot, memory,
docs, the action grammar, and a thread that survives close, reload and
tab changes — but not the threads analysis. **Yes, they do the same thing,
and they should be one.** Merge: the client passes `threads` (and the full
article set the News tab holds) to `/api/chat` when the surface is news;
the chat's system block gains the analyst's two persona lines and the
more/less-of preference instruction; the News tab's rail becomes a docked
view of the same `ChatPanel` (same thread), and the thread cards' "Ask about
this" seeds it. Retire `NewsChatPanel` and `/api/news-chat` (one fewer Opus
route, one fewer ledger label). Token cost is a wash: the assistant already
sends articles; threads add ~1.5k input tokens on this surface only, inside
the cacheable block.

**N11 — Threads do not reach Glance or the brief.** *Build, S–M.* Glance's
Breaking & critical is the curated article set; the brief prompt gets
trend movers but not thread trajectories. Two cheap joins once N2/N4 exist:
a "rising threads" line in the brief prompt (label, run, headline — no new
model call, the brief is already paid for), and the ⚑ followed threads as
rows on Glance's Breaking panel when they are rising. Deliberately not a
Threads tile on the status row: a thread is reading, not status.

### Decisions needed

1. **Threads as the News landing view** (N1) — recommended yes, with the
   3-per-day generation cap.
2. **Fold History into Threads** (N2) — recommended yes; History's three
   sub-tabs become the Moving rail, the card trajectories and the date
   picker. Nothing is deleted from the store.
3. **Merge the News analyst into the assistant** (N10) — recommended yes.
4. **Newsletter default = earned rows only** (N8) — recommended yes;
   "show all" is one click and the digest sees everything.
