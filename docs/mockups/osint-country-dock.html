What changes · option A

  1. 1The selected country is a highlighted ROW (sky bar), not a container. The rows below it never move — Russia's room no longer pushes Germany two screens down.
  2. 2One distinct room panel under the list. Its header carries the command's countries as tabs and ‹ › so you walk EUCOM without scrolling; ✕ or Esc closes. The map below still follows the open country.
  3. 3The room's sections become tabs (Overview · Incidents · News · Civil · Health · Spectrum · Airfields) with counts, so the panel is one screen tall instead of five. Overview = posture line, conflict banner, AI SITREP, civil, the country's airfields.
  4. ✓Smallest change: `CountryRoom` moves from inside the row to after the list; the row keeps its data. Trade-off: on a long country list the room is still below the fold of the list.
