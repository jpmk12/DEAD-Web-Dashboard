What changes · option C (recommended)

  1. 1A country row is a DOOR (→), not a container. The board page is lists only, so it never jumps; the row keeps its data and the highlight shows what is open.
  2. 2The room slides in from the right (62 % of the width on desktop, full-screen on a phone), with a breadcrumb, ‹ › through the command's countries, ⇥ pin (docks it as a right column on ≥1536 px — option B without a second layout), ✕ / Esc. The URL carries ?room=country:Germany, so ⌘K, Glance tiles and the primer's doors open the same thing.
  3. 3Tabs replace the five-screen scroll: Overview (posture · conflict banner · AI SITREP · civil · the country's airfields · incidents summary) · Incidents · News · Civil · Health · Spectrum · Airfields.
  4. 4The pinned field chips on the row, the Airfields tab, the My airfields cards and the new Airfields-by-command rows all open the SAME drawer on its airfield page (the full SITREP) — no field is ever more than one tap away, from anywhere.
  5. ✓Trade-off: the map behind is half covered while the drawer is open. The room has its own incident mini-map, the big map still follows the open subject, and ⇥ pin keeps both visible on a wide monitor.
