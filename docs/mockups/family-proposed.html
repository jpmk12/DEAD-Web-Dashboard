Proposed changes — callouts

  1. 1An attention line replaces the subtitle: what needs you, what has no date, what lands while you are away, what is waiting to be filed, when the digest last updated and whether anything is new since your last visit. The tab checks for new mail every 10 minutes while open (a Gmail list, no model call) and re-reads only when new messages exist.
  2. 2Done and Not-mine rows leave the list into one folded line with undo; the counts reload. The same obligation from several newsletters is one row (“×2 newsletters”), as on the Calendar.
  3. 3Set date / Later is one menu: the dates the email itself names are offered as one-tap chips with the sentence they came from, or pick a date, or snooze. A date you set is yours; a later extraction never overwrites it. Nothing is assigned automatically.
  4. 4Each kid’s brief is a running brief: the previous text plus only the NEW mail goes to the model, which updates it (keeps what stands, drops what passed, adds what is new). Lines first seen after your last visit carry a “new” chip; the header says when it updated. “＋ Add” on each dated line replaces the separate Dates found list.
  5. 5A person with no mail this fortnight says so, with the last noted item, instead of an empty card; a failed model call says “summary unavailable” rather than going blank.
  6. 6While you are away names the trip once, splits “happened since you left” from “before you are back”, groups by kid, and can be copied as a list for the call. Past trips no longer match.
  7. 7A proposal shows the email it came from and the sentence around the mention, not a raw link, and says what tracking will do.
  8. 8Proposals and discovery move below the content; discovery stays folded with its last-scan line.
