⌂ Household bills, documents & wellbeing

Organised around one idea: the things that hurt you are the ones with no alarm attached. Autopay reminds itself; a manual bill does not. A subscription renews silently — that is the problem, not the exception. A passport expires with no notice at all. And a bill that stops arriving gives no signal by definition, which is why silence gets its own panel. Same rule as the rest of the app: nothing here is guessed, and every row says where it came from.
