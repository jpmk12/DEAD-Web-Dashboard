What is live in this prototype
Drill in place — command → boards + countries → situation room + airfields → SITREP. Second click collapses. Other rows stay.
★ must-tracks — on any row and in the panel. Re-sorts the board (★ first, then worst), the primer tie-break, the airfield strip, the AOR chips, and which airfields get a full SITREP (6 slots).
Primer doors — open exactly that command / country / airfield and scroll to it.
Map follows the board — markers outside the open command dim, the force rail narrows, the events list narrows, the chip reads “following the board”. Any AOR chip overrides until you drill again.
Lenses — “near my airfields” (events within reach of a ★ field) and “new since look”.
Sample data — the numbers, drivers and prose are illustrative. Feeds, Sources, View, Layers, Legend, Demand read and the AI read are inert.
