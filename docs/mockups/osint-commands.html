Proposed changes — callouts (landing)

  1. 1Three panes, not four: Commands is the landing (Watch and Regional fold into it); Feeds and Sources stay as secondary chips. The map lives inside Commands.
  2. 2Where to look first: a ranked, deterministic primer over what the app already computes (posture escalations, boards at warning/alert, bases worse than yesterday, demand rising, your last look, forming convergence, calls due) — each line with a door into the board. “✦ Read” spends a model call only on tap.
  3. 3My airfields: hub, spokes and ★ must-track fields with their five LEDs and driver, pinned above the board so a SITREP is one click from anywhere regardless of command.
  4. 4The command board: one row per combatant command — I&W, posture, bases, demand, events, Δ since your last look — ★ must-tracks first, then worst. Each row says WHY it is coloured in one line.
  5. 5A ★ on any command, country or airfield declares it a must-track (stored in the Mission Profile). ★ rows sort first and never fold; ★ airfields earn a full SITREP (6 slots); the primer weights them; the map’s “My AO” is the ★ commands.
  6. 6Commands you do not track fold into one quiet line — present, never hidden; a quiet command still says “absence of signal, not evidence of calm”.
  7. 7The map is retained whole; its AOR filter follows the board (open a command → the map flies there and the force rail and events narrow to it) and can still be set by hand.
  8. 8Significant events keeps its reach legs, grouped by command and following the board, with a “near my airfields” lens and new-since-your-last-look markers.
