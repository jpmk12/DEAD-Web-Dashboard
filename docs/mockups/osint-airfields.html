What changes · airfields

  1. 1A′: one register of every tracked field, grouped by command with a worst-field line per group, filter chips, "hide green", ＋ Track…. Sits between the commands and the picture. The Weather tab's by-command grid becomes a view of the same data.
  2. 2The row carries what you asked for last time — hub / spoke switch, ★, ✕ — so changing a field never means finding its country first.
  3. 3B′: inside a command, Airfields is a peer of Countries (flat, one level up). Cheaper than A′ but still nested under the command, and no cross-command list.
  4. 4C′: the SITREP is the room's airfield page, with tabs (SITREP · Weather · Ops · Threats · Infra · Spectrum · LIMFACs · History) and ‹ › through the command's fields. Every ICAO in the app becomes a door to it.
