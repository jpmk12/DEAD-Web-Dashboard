SITREP ops timeline — scrubbing and placement options

The same fixture drives every option: ETAD, Thu 26 Nov 0900Z, with freezing rain on a cold-soaked field, snow behind it, a dry, freezing Friday night and a second snow band on Saturday. RWY 05/23 is closed daily 1400–1800Z for works. Move the mouse along any timeline or drag a finger across it. Click or tap to pin the cursor, and click again to release it.

Scrubbing — three ways to show the hour under the cursor

A · Time flaglightest

A hairline crosses every lane, with a Zulu flag on the axis (local time beside it). Only the lane under the pointer shows its value, as a small tag. Nothing moves the page, but you read one lane at a time, and on a phone your finger covers the tag.

B · Flag + hour readoutrecommended

The same hairline and Zulu flag, plus a fixed readout under the chart: the date-time group (DTG), local time, hours from now, whether you are inside a limiting window and when it clears, then every lane's value at that hour, including temperature, dew point, feels-like and freezing level. Your finger never covers it. With the readout carrying the words, a phone can show all 72 h at once instead of scrolling sideways.

C · Readout + step through changesfor hunting

B, plus step buttons that jump the cursor to the next or previous change in any lane (a bar starting or ending, a 0 °C crossing) or to the next limiting window, and a line naming the next change. ← → step one hour; Shift + arrow steps to the next change. This is the fastest way to walk a 72-h period for problems without reading every bar.

+ The same band on every airfield

For comparing fields: add the one-line 72-h band to each card in My airfields and to each row in the airfield register, so "which of my fields has a problem Thursday night?" is answered without opening each room. This can sit alongside 2 or 3.

Also in every option: a green now line that moves with the clock, the shaded light conditions, and the dashed "TAF ends" line. The scrubber reads only what the timeline already holds; it adds no fetch and no model call.
